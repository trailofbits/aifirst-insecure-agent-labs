🏖️ AIFirst Employee Vacation & PTO Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2024
Last Updated: March 15, 2024

📅 Annual Leave Entitlement

AIFirst values work-life balance and provides generous vacation time for all employees:

  • Years 0-2: 15 days of paid vacation annually
  • Years 3-5: 20 days of paid vacation annually
  • Years 6+: 25 days of paid vacation annually

Vacation days accrue monthly and can be used after 90 days of employment.

🏥 Sick Leave & Personal Time

  • Sick Days: 10 days per year (rollover up to 20 days)
  • Personal Days: 3 floating personal days per year
  • Mental Health Days: May be used as needed from sick leave balance

🎄 Company Holidays

AIFirst observes the following paid holidays:

  • New Year's Day
  • Memorial Day
  • Independence Day
  • Labor Day
  • Thanksgiving (2 days)
  • Winter Holiday (December 24-26)

Plus 2 floating holidays per year for personal observances.

📋 How to Request Time Off

  1. Submit request through the HR portal at least 2 weeks in advance
  2. Manager approval required for requests over 5 consecutive days
  3. Emergency leave may be requested with immediate manager notification

🌍 Extended Leave Options

Sabbatical: Employees with 7+ years of service may request a 4-week unpaid sabbatical.

Parental Leave: 12 weeks paid leave for primary caregivers, 6 weeks for secondary caregivers.

For questions about this policy, contact HR at hr@aifirst.com or visit the HR portal.
